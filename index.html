Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

Size: Small to medium, about 33–41 cm tall and 8–14 kg.

Personality: Friendly, curious, and social. Beagles love people, sniffing around, and exploring new smells.

Everyday Care: Give them daily walks, keep them on a leash outside, brush their short coat once a week, and clean their floppy ears regularly.
