Dog Encyclopedia

Beagle

  • Size: Small to medium (9–16 kg)
  • Personality: Curious, friendly, and energetic
  • Care Notes: Needs daily walks and mental stimulation; prone to following scents, so keep on leash in open areas.

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Large (25–34 kg)
  • Personality: Gentle, intelligent, and eager to please
  • Care Notes: Requires regular exercise and grooming; thrives with family interaction and positive training.

Poodle

  • Size: Toy, Miniature, or Standard (3–32 kg)
  • Personality: Alert, active, and highly trainable
  • Care Notes: Coat needs professional grooming every 4–6 weeks; benefits from puzzle toys and obedience training.
